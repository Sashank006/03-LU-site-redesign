Meet Fran

Francine Miller opened her first stall on Valencia Street in 1995 with a folding table and three buckets of carnations. Thirty years later she still cuts most of the arrangements herself and knows a surprising number of customers by the flowers they buy.

Fran grew up in the Mission and never left. Ten percent of every sale goes to Bay Area homelessness organizations, a decision she made in her first year and has never revisited.
